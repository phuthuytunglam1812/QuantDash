# W3-12 — Usability test protocol (3–5 participants)

## Participant profile

Recruit 3–5 people with beginner or early-intermediate investing knowledge. Do
not coach them through the tasks. Ask permission before recording screen/audio.

## 20-minute session

1. **First impression (2 min):** “What do you think this product helps you do?”
2. **Screen (4 min):** Find companies with positive signal, P/E <= 35, and
   profit margin >= 10%. Explain why missing P/E cannot pass.
3. **Compare (3 min):** Use the RSI/P-E map and select one ticker to inspect.
4. **Understand (4 min):** Explain adjusted close, RSI, volatility, and the
   ticker’s overall signal using the available help.
5. **Market Quest (5 min):** Complete one lesson, game, inspection, and
   multi-company allocation.
6. **Debrief (2 min):** What was confusing, trustworthy, useful, or missing?

## Metrics

- Task completion without help: yes/no
- Time per task
- Misinterpretations of percentile, P/E, beta, signal, and missing data
- Number of dead ends or unexpected loops
- Single Ease Question after each task (1 very difficult – 7 very easy)
- Final usefulness and trust rating (1–5)
